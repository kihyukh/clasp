BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few useful details.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Clasp uses an account for your private Personal workspace and the shared workspaces you can access. Sign in inside the app to get started.

Can I work offline?

In the desktop app, previously downloaded workspaces and initialized notes open from the local cache. Edits save locally while disconnected. Initial sign-in, a workspace’s first download, and synchronization need an internet connection.

Can I use my existing papers and notes?

You can import local PDFs, scholarly links, or an existing Obsidian vault. Your notes use Markdown, and highlights can be exported as XFDF or in an annotated PDF.

Which computer should I download for?

The universal Mac DMG supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later. The Windows installer is for 64-bit Intel and AMD PCs running Windows 10 or later. A native Windows ARM build is not included in this release.

Where can I report a problem?

Open an issue in the Clasp downloads repository. Include your operating system, Clasp version, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Please leave private papers and account information out of public reports.
